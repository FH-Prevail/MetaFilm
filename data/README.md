# Data

## Monash CarParts (included)

`carparts/car_parts_dataset_without_missing_values.tsf`: 2,674 intermittent
monthly series of car-part sales (January 1998 to March 2002), from the Monash
Time Series Forecasting Archive (Zenodo record 4656021), distributed there under
the CC BY 4.0 licence. Missing values were replaced by zeros by the archive.

- R. Godahewa, C. Bergmeir, G. I. Webb, R. J. Hyndman and P. Montero-Manso
  (2021). Monash Time Series Forecasting Archive. NeurIPS Datasets and
  Benchmarks Track.
- R. J. Hyndman (2015). expsmooth: Data Sets from "Forecasting with
  Exponential Smoothing". R package version 2.3.

CarParts has no categorical metadata; in the paper it serves as a control
panel on which metadata conditioning should have no effect.
